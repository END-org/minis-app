## Objective

Investigate production deployment and release-readiness options for MINIS within a seven-working-day research window, for a release expected in approximately two months.

## Workstreams

1. Firebase versus Supabase Authentication
2. Deployment, PostgreSQL, and Object Storage
3. Upload and MVP Video Delivery
4. Android and iOS Release Readiness

## Scope Rules

- Compare no more than three serious options for one decision.
- A maximum of two experiments are mandatory.
- The mandatory experiments are deployment and object storage.
- An authentication experiment is conditional, not automatically required.
- Do not implement production infrastructure.
- Do not publish the final mobile apps.
- Provide one primary recommendation and one fallback.
- Collect company budget, ownership, expected workload, and release requirements from the relevant stakeholders.

## Required Deliverables

- Stakeholder inputs.
- Provider/option comparisons.
- Decision matrix.
- Cost model for Pilot, Expected Launch, and Higher Growth.
- Research log.
- Required experiment reports.
- Mobile release checklist.
- Final report.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Every recommendation cites official sources or reproducible experiment evidence.
- Assumptions are explicit and dated.
- Cost model includes uncertainty and confidence.
- Migration impact is mapped back to current MINIS application areas.
- Unresolved questions are separated from recommendations.
